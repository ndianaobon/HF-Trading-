FAQ

Common questions

More answers in the full FAQ and Help Center.

Where do HarborFinance prices come from?+
Prices are streamed from our configured market-data provider. If the feed is delayed or unavailable, the interface labels it clearly and trading against stale prices is blocked.
Are investment plan returns guaranteed?+
No. Plans are managed strategies with documented risk. Results can be positive or negative and are only known when a subscription is settled.
What is demo mode?+
Demo mode lets you explore the full platform with simulated balances. Demo deposits, withdrawals and fills are labelled and never move real funds.
How are withdrawals protected?+
Withdrawals require step-up verification (2FA code or password), reserve your funds immediately, and pass review before they're processed.
