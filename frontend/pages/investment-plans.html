Investment plans

Managed strategies. Transparent terms.

Allocate to a professionally managed strategy with a documented approach, fixed duration and published fees. Every plan carries risk, and your result may be lower than your allocation.

How plans work

From subscription to settlement.

What happens when I subscribe?+
Your allocation is reserved from your USDT balance and the subscription is marked Pending until the next allocation window, when it becomes Active.
How is my result calculated?+
At the end of the term the strategy's realised profit or loss attributable to your allocation is recorded. Management fees (pro-rated) and performance fees (on positive results only) are deducted before the balance is returned to your wallet.
Can I exit early?+
Plans that allow early exit can be cancelled at any time. Pending subscriptions are refunded in full; active subscriptions are returned minus the plan's early-exit fee and pro-rated management fees.
Can I lose money?+
Yes. Strategies can lose value, and the amount returned to you can be less than your allocation.
