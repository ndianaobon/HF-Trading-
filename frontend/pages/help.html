How we protect your account

  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id; we never store or see them in plain text.
  • Two-factor authentication with time-based codes and single-use backup codes.
  • Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. You can end any session from Settings.
  • Sensitive data such as 2FA secrets and ID numbers is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • Withdrawals require step-up verification and are reviewed before processing.
  • Balances can never go negative: every balance change runs in a database transaction with integrity constraints.
  • Administrative actions on accounts and funds are recorded in an audit log.
  • Rate limiting and request-origin checks protect against brute force and cross-site attacks.
