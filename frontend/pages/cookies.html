Cookie Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

This policy describes the cookies and similar technologies HarborFinance uses. We keep them to the minimum needed to run a secure service.

1. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the services to work and cannot be switched off.

  • hf_session — keeps you signed in. HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production. Expires after 12 hours, or 30 days if you choose “Remember me”.

2. Local storage

We may store interface preferences, such as your last selected chart timeframe and indicators, in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

3. Analytics and advertising

HarborFinance does not currently use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If this changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser settings. Deleting the session cookie signs you out.
