Automated bot trading

Set your rules. Let the bot place the orders.

Create bots that buy or sell on a schedule, or act the moment a coin reaches your price. Every order runs through the same trading engine as a manual trade, and you can pause or stop a bot at any time.

Recurring bot (DCA)

Buy or sell a fixed USDT amount every hour, 4 hours, day or week. Set a number of runs or let it continue until you stop it.

Example: buy 50 USDT of BTC every week, 12 times.

Price-trigger bot

Place one order automatically when a coin rises to or above, or falls to or below, the price you choose. The bot checks the live price every 15 seconds.

Example: buy 200 USDT of ETH if it falls to 2,400.

How bots work

From setup to your order history.

  1. 1

    Choose a market and a rule

    Pick any crypto market, buy or sell, the amount per order, and a schedule or trigger price.

  2. 2

    The bot places market orders

    Each run is a normal market order from your wallet at the current price, with the usual trading fee, and appears in your order history.

  3. 3

    You stay in control

    You get a notification after every run. Pause, resume or stop a bot at any time. If an order can't be placed, for example because your balance is too low, the bot pauses and tells you why.
